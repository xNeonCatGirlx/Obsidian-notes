Causes of Viscosity in Gases
- As the molecules of gas are not rigidly constrained, and cohesive forces are small, there will be a continuous interchange of molecules between adjacent layers which are travelling at different velocities. //

Kinematic Viscosity ( v [ m^2/s])
- Defined as the ratio : u/pv
- Gives measure of relative influence of viscosity over inertia in flows
Surface Tension (σ [N/m])
- The property of liquid which enables insects to walk on the surface of water, σ is defined as the force per unit length, so unites are N/m.

Fluid Properties
	Surface tension
- Its effect is to reduce the surface to a minimum
- Thus, drops of liquid tend to be spherical, causing for small droplets an increase of internal pressure.
- The forces caused by internal pressure are F = p x πr^2
