## Part 9 - Thermodynamic Reversibility
Non equilibrium process: Large heat source next to a system, large thermal temperature gradient
Equilibrium process: Range of heat sources which differ by a tiny amount so that the source is never greater than an 'infinitesimally' small amount so there are a series of equilibrium states

Carnot efficiency = Maximum efficiency

Energy leaves hot temperature source which *decreases entropy*
Some energy enters cold sink *increases entropy*

## Part 10 - Entropy
Recap on ENTROPY  
- Entropy arises as a consequence of the second law in a similar way internal energy arises from the first law  
- Often expressed as a measure of the randomness or disorder. The loss of available work.  
- Derived from ancient Greek for ‘transformation’  
- Reversible adiabatic systems – no change in entropy (isentropic)  
- Irreversible systems – increase in entropy
