Pure Translation:
During translational motion, the body is the only translating and there is no rotation. Therefore there can be no relative motion between different points within the body and all points in the body must have the same linear velocity and acceleration.

Rectilinear Translation:
The body is only translating along a straight line

General Plane Motion:
Translation of whole body + Rotation about A
Given the translational motion of one point in the rigid body and the rotational motion of the rigid body, the motion of any other point can be worked out using the relative motion relationships.
