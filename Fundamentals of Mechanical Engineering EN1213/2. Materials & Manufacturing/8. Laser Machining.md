Basics of Lasers
- Light - electromagnetic radiation of any frequency, not only visible light.
- Laser is a coherent, collimated, and monochromatic beam of light with wavelength raging from ultraviolet to infrared.
==Three Key Properties==
- Monochromatic - A single wavelength
- Collimated - stays narrow over long distances
- Coherent - all waves in phase

==How the beam is created==
- The lamp pumps energy into the crystal
- Excited atoms release light that triggers more identical light
- Mirrors bounce the light back and forth, amplifying it
- Part escapes through the partially transmitting mirror: the beam

Laser Material INteractions
- Vaporisation
- Melting
- Curing
- Heating
