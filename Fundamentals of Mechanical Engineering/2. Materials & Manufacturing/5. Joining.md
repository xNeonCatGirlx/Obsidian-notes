Why us e 'joining' processes
- Assembly of parts - engineering system are complex
- Individual parts are easier to manufacture
- Easier maintenance - products can be disassembled
- Allows use of different materials in one system
- Promotes mass production and reduces cost

Mechanical joining
- Bolt, nut, screw, pin, rivit, stitch, staple, shrink fit, forge fit, seam, snap-in fixing

Adhesive bonding
- Polymeric adhesive

Brazing/Soldering
- Filler with lower melting point than base metal

Welding
- Weld joint is similar to. same as the base metal
- Solid or liquid state
- With or without filler

Solid satte welding:
- Heat and pressure applied or pressure alone applied below melting temperature of the material
- Filler material not usually used
- Requires clean mating faces to allow atomic bonding to occur
- Uses only heat to form weld
- Carried out above melting point of materials
- Possible with and without filler material
- Numerous processes avaliable
