A two force body:
If only two forces act upon a body in equilibrium, the forces must be equal and opposite and share the same line of action.
A three force body:
If only three forces act upon a body in equilibrium, their lines of action must intersect at a single point, or they must be parallel.

Simple Trusses
For a simple truss the number of members M, and the number of joints N, are related by the equation M = 2N - 3
