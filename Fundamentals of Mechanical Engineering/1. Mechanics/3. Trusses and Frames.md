A two force body:
If only two forces act upon a body in equilibrium, the forces must be equal and opposite and share the same line of action.
A three force body:
If only three forces act upon a body in equilibrium, their lines of action must intersect at a single point, or they must be parallel.

Simple Trusses
For a simple truss the number of members M, and the number of joints N, are related by the equation M = 2N - 3

## Method for Truss
Method of join for solving truss problems
- Assign a tension to each member
- Solve for reactions at supports by considering truss as a whole rigid body
- Look at each joint separately - choose simple joints first
- Use last equations as a check
Negative values of tension equal compression

Method of sections
- Solve for equilibrium of truss as a whole to find reactions at supports
- Section the truss: Pass an imaginary section through the truss that cut 3 members AT MOST  
- Consider each part of the sectioned truss as a separate free body – can then find the forces in the 3 cut members  
- Repeat as necessary
