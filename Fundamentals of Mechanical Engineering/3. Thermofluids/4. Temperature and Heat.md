==Temperature is a measure of the energy associated with molecular motion of particles of a substance.==

- Absolute temperature measured in Kevin and is obtained from thermodynamic theory

==Heat is a form of energy transfer due to a difference in temperature at the boundary of a thermodynamic system==
- There cannot be heat transfer between two systems that are at the same temperature
- Heat flow is the transfer of thermal energy, not transfer of a fluid 
- Heat flow is energy in transition
- An adiabatic process is one where no heat transfer occurs. However, it's important to note that the temperature can change by other means
