Q. In a closed cycle, the first law states that the net work transfer is proportional to what?
A. The first law of thermodynamics states that for a closed system undergoing a cycle, the net work transfer ($\oint dW$) is directly proportional to the net heat transfer ($\oint dQ$).

Q. In a thermodynamic system what is the term used to describe the energy either lost or gained in the form of work and heat?
A. Work and heat are forms of **energy in transit**. The net energy gained or lost that remains stored within the system is the change in **Internal Energy** ($\Delta U$).

Q. What is the name of the equation used to describe energy transfer in a closed system?
A. **Non-Flow Energy Equation (NFEE)**: The equation describing energy transfer in a closed system is $Q = \Delta U + W$ (or $Q - W = \Delta U$).

Q. In a thermodynamic system, is the internal energy a property or not?
A. **Yes, it is a property**: Internal energy is a **thermodynamic property** (state function). 

Q. What is the constant J?  
A. **Joule's Mechanical Equivalent of Heat**: The constant $J$ (Joule's constant) is the conversion factor relating units of mechanical work to heat energy ($W = J \cdot Q$). In the SI unit system where both work and heat are measured in Joules, $J = 1$. In traditional engineering units, $J \approx 4.184 \text{ J/cal}$ or $778.16 \text{ ft}\cdot\text{lbf/BTU}$.
