1st Law of thermodynamics: 
- Total amount of energy stays the same
- ⨊ (δQ – δW) = 0 (In a closed system)
- ⨊Q – ⨊W = 0

2nd Law:
Heat cannot flow from cold to hot 
3rd Law:
There is an absolute minimum temperature 


**Non flow energy equation**
Q - W = U2 - U1 (U is internal energy)

==The net work transfer in a closed system cycle is proportional to the net heat transfer==
⨊δQ ∝ ⨊δW   (Circle in the centre of sigma means a cycle)
J⨊δQ = ⨊δW  J = The mechanical equivalent of heat
