**What is the study of thermodynamics?**
The relationship between the **properties** of a **substance and the quantities** of work & heat which cause a change of thermodynamic **state**

T – Temperature (K)  
H – Enthalpy (kJ)  
U - Internal energy (kJ)  
P - Pressure (bar or N/m2)  
V – Volume (m^3)  
S – Entropy (kJ/K)

Uppercase - Absolute quantities
Lower case - Specific quantities

Fluids - Liquid, Gas, Vapour

**Thermodynamics System**
A region in space containing matter whose behaviour we are investigating

1. Isolated system
- No exchange of matter
- No exchange of heat
2. Closed system
- No exchange of matter
- It can exchange heat
3. Open system
- It can exchange matter
- It can exchange heat
4. Steady system 
- Steady flow in a system
5. Unsteady system
- Irregular flow in a system

Heat is the transfer of energy

Processes on a fluid in a **closed system** are **Non-Flow Process**
Processes on a fluid in an **open system** are **Flow Process**

The Thermodynamic **state** of a system at a given moment is determined by the values of its **properties** at that moment

- The thermodynamic state depends on the properties
- The change in property is the **difference** between initial and final values
- The value of properties are **irrespective** of the intermediate states

**Equilibrium**
Thermodynamic equilibrium is achieved in a system when no further changes take place if it were isolated from its surroundings
If the fluid is in thermal equilibrium during the process it is reversible process
If the fluid is not in thermal equilibrium during the process, then it is defined as  a non-reversible process
If two bodies are at equal temperature to a third body, then they are also equal in temperature to each other

Work appears at the boundary when a system changes its state due to movement by the system
Work is done on the system when work is transferred at the system boundary
