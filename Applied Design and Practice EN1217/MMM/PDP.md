### Was the role play exercise something you found easy or was it rather awkward? Why?

I found it quite easy to role play the exercise. Being able to understand the scenario from another view is definitely helpful. I was able to find more information in this kind of situation. 

### How easily could you empathise with the three parties in the exercise (engineer / project manager / owner)? Why?

I could empathise with all the parties in every scenario, all of the parties have their own point. For example, the bike brakes scenario. The engineer wants to verify the new brakes are working and use statical data to persuade the owner. The project manager is agreeing on both sides. And the owner knows that they need to sell those bikes as soon as possible to keep the company running.

### Do you think an undergraduate should be worried  about communication and ethics – or is this someone else’s problem?
Definitely, it is a good idea to start going through those potential scenarios as the real world scenarios is much more complicated than this and would need to conduct a lot more consideration.

### How would you feel if you were exposed to an ethical dilemma such as the one in the roleplay? Do you think they are common?
I would consider the view for everyone and try to think of a solution that is more balanced towards everyone instead of just one party. I think this kind of situation in very common in real life.
