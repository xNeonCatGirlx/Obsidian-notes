Types of IP
- Trade secret - Type of intellectual property that protects confidential business information and gives a company a competitive advantage
- Copyright - Protects the expression of original literary or artistic work (including web content)
- Trademarks - Protects the words and logos used to distinguish your products from those of your competitors
- Design Right - Protects the appearance of a product
- Patents - protect novel inventions, products and processes

1.Trade secrets
- Trade secrets consist of confidential business information which provides and enterprise with a competitive edge
- They do not come with any formal means of protection other than Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) or similar contractual arrangements.

2.Copyright
- Copyright gives creators of original works the right to stop others from copying that work without permission
- Lasts for the life of the author + 70 Years
- This right arises automatically in the UK
- Original works includes
	- Literary works - Computer programs, manuals, articles, commercial docs
	- Artistic works - Technical drawings
	- Dramatic work, music, films, sound recordings, typographical arrangements of published editions

3.Trademarks
- These protect a brand
- Symbol means that this is a registered trademark
- ™ Symbol means that this is being used as a trademark, but is not registered
- Trademarks can last forever as long as a fee is paid every 10 years
- Do not want your company or product to become the generic name for your product 
- If you set up a new business such as a start up company, you should seek advice on trademarks

4.Design Rights
- A design is the outward appearance of the whole or parts of a product.
- A product can be any industrial or handcraft items.
- Examples of design features include lines, colours and shapes
- Examples of the products to which they are applied or in which they are incorporated include packaging and logos.
- There are two requirements for protections
	- Novelty, the design must be new
	- Individual character, This requirement is not met if another design which creates the same overall impression on the informed user has already been disclosed.

5.Patents
- Protect the way something works
- Patents are directed to technical features - not appearance, brand, artistic stuff
- Can be a product, a method, a chemical composition, a kit of parts
- Monopoly right
- Let you stop other people form making, selling, importing or using your inventions
- Lasts 20 years from filing date, if annual fee is paid
- Territorial - there is no worldwide patent
